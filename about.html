About Lesotho

A small kingdom with extraordinary beauty and culture.

THE MOUNTAIN KINGDOM

A Land of Beauty and Tradition

Lesotho is a mountainous country in Southern Africa, known for its dramatic landscapes, high-altitude scenery and distinctive Basotho heritage.

Visitors can discover traditional villages, explore mountain trails, admire waterfalls and experience the hospitality of local communities.

Our Mission

To inspire visitors to explore Lesotho responsibly while appreciating its natural beauty and cultural heritage.
